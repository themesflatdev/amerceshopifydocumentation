  • About Amerce

    What is Amerce?

    Amerce is a modern, feature-rich Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme built for general commerce, fashion, and lifestyle stores. Built on top of Shopify's Skeleton Theme foundation and heavily extended with mega menu navigation, quick add/quick view, wishlist, compare, product bought-together, bundle & discount sections, and a marquee announcement bar. Amerce helps merchants build a fast, conversion-focused storefront without any build step — every asset is hand-written and served directly by Shopify.

    Key Features

    • Mega menu navigation: Multi-column dropdown menus with menu links, product lists, and image cards.
    • Quick add & Quick view: Let customers add to cart or preview full product details without leaving the current page.
    • Wishlist & Compare: Customers can save favorite products and compare products side by side.
    • Bought together & complementary products: Increase average order value with smart product recommendations powered by Shopify Search & Discovery.
    • Bundle, discount & urgency sections: Bundle save, discount banners, countdown timers, urgency badges, live-viewer counts, and stock progress bars.
    • Advanced product cards: 6 card layout styles, swatch hover preview, badges & tags, and product ratings.
    • Powerful cart: Drawer or page cart type, cart recommendations, free-shipping progress bar, gift wrap, order notes, shipping estimator, and discount code field.
    • Facets & filtering: Collection filters and smart search powered by Shopify Search & Discovery.
    • Marquee announcement bar & top bar: Highlight promotions across your store.
    • Multi-language & multi-currency ready: Built for Shopify Markets and Translate & Adapt.
    • Shopify Online Store 2.0: Flexible drag-and-drop customization with sections and theme blocks.
    • Mobile-first, SEO & performance optimized: Fast loading and search engine friendly.

  • Amerce Theme Installation

    Upload and publish a Home design

    Follow the steps below to install a chosen Home design of the Amerce Shopify Theme on your online store:

    1. 1. Log in to your Shopify Admin and navigate to Online Store → Themes.
    2. 2. Under Theme Library, click Add theme → Upload zip file.
    3. 3. Choose the .zip file for the Home design you selected (see What you receive above) and click Upload.
    4. 4. Wait for Shopify to finish uploading and processing the theme file.
    5. 5. The theme now appears in your Theme Library as an unpublished theme. Click Customize to preview and adjust content, or Preview to see it without editing.
    6. 6. When you're ready to make it live, open the theme's Actions menu → Publish.

    Note:  Shopify lets you keep several themes in your Theme Library at once, but only one theme can be Published (live) at a time. Unpublished themes can still be freely previewed and edited.

    Setting up store content after install

    A freshly uploaded theme only contains the demo layout — it does not include your own products, collections, pages, menus, or images. After publishing, continue with the guides in this documentation to add your real store content:

    1. •  Theme Settings — logo, colors, typography, product cards, cart.
    2. •  Require Apps — install the apps the theme depends on for search/filtering, translations, and reviews.
    3. •  Collection & Products, Blogs, Menu Config, Pages — recreate the demo content with your own products/pages/menus.
    4. •  Metafields — required for blocks like Live viewers, Countdown timer, Stock progress bar, Urgency badge, and Bought together.

  • Require Apps

  • Recommended Apps

    Amerce relies on the third-party apps listed below to support specific enhanced functionalities. These apps are not bundled with the theme by default. To use those features, you will need to install each required app manually from the Shopify App Store.

    Shopify Search & Discovery

    A great shopping experience starts with simple search and seamless discovery. Customize search, filtering, and product recommendations to enhance your products' relevance and boost sales. Use the Shopify Search & Discovery app to power the theme's facets (collection filters), predictive search, and the Bought together / Complementary products block on the product page. Additionally, employ analytics to gain insights into how customers navigate your store, helping shoppers find exactly what they’re looking for.

    • •  Customize filters to let shoppers refine their search by multiple categories.
    • •  Make synonym groups, so product descriptions match the terms shoppers use.
    • •  Use product boosts to feature products you want highlighted in search results.
    • •  Add related and complementary products on product detail pages.
    • •  Access analytics to understand your store’s search and discovery performance.

    Shopify Translate & Adapt

    Translate & Adapt lets you auto-translate up to two languages for free, and manually add as many translations as you wish. The side-by-side editor makes it easy to compare and review your translations, and can also be used to adjust content between markets that speak the same language to ensure cultural, seasonal, and regional relevance.

    • •  Manually add, edit, and review translations with the side-by-side editor.
    • •  Auto-translate 2 languages with Google Translate (cloud.google.com/translate).
    • •  Adapt content to reflect spelling and messaging variations between markets.
    • •  Localize directly from the theme editor, or any resource page in your admin.
    • •  Manage localized store content seamlessly from your Shopify admin.

    Judge.me Product Reviews

    Amerce uses Judge.me Product Reviews to collect and display customer reviews. Judge.me writes its rating data into the standard reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count product metafields, which the theme reads directly to show the star-rating badge on product cards, the product page, and Featured product sections — no extra theme configuration is required once Judge.me has synced ratings to a product.

    • •  Automatically collect reviews via post-purchase review request emails.
    • •  Let customers submit photo/video reviews from the storefront.
    • •  Show a star-rating summary and review count on product cards and the product page.
    • •  Sync the average rating to reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count so the theme can render it without any extra setup.
    • •  Import existing reviews from other platforms (Shopify Product Reviews, Loox, Yotpo, etc.) when migrating.

  • Collection & Products

  • Collection

    Collections in Shopify allow you to group products into categories, making it easier for customers to browse and find items they’re looking for. You can create collections manually by selecting specific products, or automatically using conditions such as tags, price, vendor, or product type. Once created, collections can be displayed on your homepage, menus, or any page throughout your store. Properly organized collections not only improve navigation but also enhance the overall shopping experience.

    1. Create a Collection

    Go to Products > Collections in your Shopify admin and click Create collection. Enter a title, description,image, choose product and save

    2. Add Products to the Collection

    3. Display the Collection on Your Store

    You can show collections on your homepage, add them to your navigation menu, or link them in any page section that supports collections.

  • Products

    Products are where you manage all the items in your Shopify store. Here, you can add product titles, descriptions, images, pricing, inventory, and variants such as size or color. Proper product management helps keep your store organized and makes shopping easier for customers.

    How to Add Products Manually

    You can add products one by one directly inside Shopify:

    1. 1. Go to Products > Add product.
    2. 2. Enter the Title and Description of your product.
    3. 3. Upload product images or videos.
    4. 4. Set up Pricing (price and compare-at price).
    5. 5. Configure Inventory (SKU, barcode, stock quantity).
    6. 6. If your product has options like size or color, enable Variants and add your options.
    7. 7. Set the Product status to Active so it appears on your store.
    8. 8. Click Save to finish.

    How to Import Products (CSV)

    Shopify allows you to import multiple products at once using a CSV file.

    1. 1. Go to Products > Import.
    2. 2. Click Add file and upload your CSV file.
    3. 3. Click Upload and continue.
    4. 4. Review the preview to make sure everything looks correct.
    5. 5. Click Import products to complete the process.
    Notes:
    1. •  The CSV file must follow Shopify’s required format.
    2. •  You can download a sample CSV from the import page.
    3. •  Product images in the CSV must be valid image URLs.

    Shopify Markets allows you to organize and manage how your store sells to different countries or regions. Each Market can include one or multiple countries, and it inherits the store’s default settings such as currency, catalog, domain, and languages. You can later customize these settings to create a localized experience for customers in that regio

    How to Create a Market in Shopify

    1. Go to Shopify Admin → Markets

    This is where all your markets are managed.

    2. Click “Create market”

    Shopify will open the New Market setup screen (like the one shown in your image).

    3. Enter a Market Name

    Fill in the Name field to label your Market.

    Example: Vietnam, Asia, Europe, Wholesale, etc.

    4. Add Countries or Regions

    Click Add condition under Includes, then choose the countries that belong to this Market.

    5. Review Inherited Settings

    The Market will automatically inherit the default settings from your main store, including:

    1. •  Currency → e.g. Vietnamese Dong (VND)
    2. •  Catalogs → All products
    3. •  Online Store → your store name (e.g. amerce)
    4. •  Domain / language → e.g. amerce.myshopify.com • 3 languages

    These settings can be customized later by clicking the ( + ) icon after saving the Market.

    6. Click “Save”

    Once saved, the Market will appear in your Markets list. You can then open it to further customize currency, domain, language, pricing, and other settings if needed.

  • Live Viewers

    1. Create Live Viewers Metafields

    1. •  Go to Settings → Metafields and metaobjects → Products, then click Add definition
    2. •  Enter Viewers Min as the Name (namespace custom.viewers_min), set the Type to Integer (One), then click Save.
    3. •  Repeat with Viewers Max (namespace custom.viewers_max), also Integer (One).

    2. Product setting

    Enter a lower and upper bound (e.g. 12–48). The product page and Featured product section will show a random, refreshing "X people are viewing this" counter within that range.

    3. Demo result

  • Countdown Timer

    1. Create a Countdown End Date Metafield

    1. •  Go to Settings → Metafields and metaobjects → Products, then click Add definition
    2. •  Enter Countdown End Date as the Name (namespace custom.countdown_end_date), set the Type to Date and time (One), then click Save.

    2. Product setting

    Set a future date/time. Enable the Countdown timer block on the product page or Featured product section to display a live countdown that hides automatically once it reaches zero.

    3. Demo result

  • Stock Progress Bar

    1. Create an Initial Stock Count Metafield

    1. •  Go to Settings → Metafields and metaobjects → Products, then click Add definition
    2. •  Enter Initial Stock Count as the Name (namespace custom.initial_stock_count), set the Type to Integer (One), then click Save.

    2. Product setting

    Enter the starting stock count for the launch/restock. The Stock progress bar block compares it against current inventory to display a "X sold, hurry up!" progress indicator.

    3. Demo result

  • Urgency Badge

    1. Create Urgency Metafields

    1. •  Go to Settings → Metafields and metaobjects → Products, then click Add definition
    2. •  Enter Urgency Sold Hours as the Name (namespace custom.urgency_sold_hours), set the Type to Integer (One), then click Save.
    3. •  Repeat with Urgency Sold Count (namespace custom.urgency_sold_count), also Integer (One).

    2. Product setting

    Enter how many units were sold and within how many hours. The Urgency badge block uses this to display messaging like "24 sold in the last 12 hours".

    3. Demo result

  • Bought Together

    1. Create a Bought Together Metafield

    1. •  Go to Settings → Metafields and metaobjects → Products, then click Add definition
    2. •  Enter Bought Together as the Name (namespace custom.bought_together), set the Type to Product (List) , then click Save.

    2. Product setting

    Add the products you want to recommend as a bundle. The Bought together block on the product page will let customers add the whole set to cart at once.

    3. Demo result

  • Header, Navigation & Announcements

  • Header

    1. What is the Header section?

    The Header section renders your store logo, main navigation menu, search/account/cart icons, and the Mega menu theme block. It has settings for menu alignment, dropdown trigger (hover or click), header style, sticky header, transparent header (for banners underneath), and header category style.

    2. How to edit the Header section

    1. • In the Theme Editor (Customize) → click Header (section groups → header-group).
    2. • Adjust menu alignment, sticky/transparent header, and header style in the section settings.
    3. • Attach a Mega menu block to any top-level menu item (see below) to enable a dropdown mega menu for that item.
    4. • Make the necessary adjustments → click Save.
  • Mega Menu (Header block)

    1. What is the Mega menu block?

    The Mega menu block turns a top-level menu item's dropdown into a multi-column layout. Inside a Mega menu block you can add Menu parent (column heading linked to a menu handle), Menu link (individual links), Product list (a small product carousel), and Image card (a promotional image with link) sub-blocks.

    2. How to configure the Mega menu block

    1. • In the Theme Editor → Header → click Add block → choose Mega menu.
    2. • Select which menu item (by handle) this mega menu attaches to, and pick the layout (columns).
    3. • Add Menu parent / Menu link / Product list / Image card sub-blocks to build out the dropdown content, then reorder them as needed.
    4. • Make the necessary adjustments → click Save.
  • Top bar

    1. What is the Top bar section?

    The Top bar is a slim promotional strip shown above the Header. It supports one or more Promo text blocks, each with its own text and optional link — useful for shipping promos, store hours, or a secondary utility menu.

    2. How to edit the Top bar section

    1. • In the Theme Editor → header-group → click Top bar (or Add section if not present).
    2. • Add/edit Promo text blocks with your message and link.
    3. • Make the necessary adjustments → click Save.
  • Announcement bar

    1. What is the Announcement bar section?

    The Announcement bar displays one or more Announcement blocks (text + link) that can rotate or scroll as a marquee. It also has a divider option and an "enable absolute position" setting so it can float over a transparent header.

    2. How to edit the Announcement bar section

    1. • In the Theme Editor → header-group → click Announcement bar.
    2. • Add/edit Announcement blocks; each has its own text and link.
    3. • Make the necessary adjustments → click Save.
